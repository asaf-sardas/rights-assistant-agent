# data/raw

Source pages for the vector index, one Kol Zchut page per `.txt` file
(`Title:` / `URL:` header, empty line, body). Only `*.txt` files are loaded.

**The current files are development samples**: short summaries written in our own words,
based on the Kol Zchut pages at the given URLs. They are not the original articles and
may be incomplete or out of date. Do not rely on them for real advice. They will be
replaced by pages downloaded by the Kol Zchut fetcher.
